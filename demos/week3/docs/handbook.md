# Acme Field Services handbook (sample data for the week 3 demo)

## Expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days in the finance portal. Meals are reimbursed up to 50 EUR per day when travelling.
Receipts are required for anything over 25 EUR.

## Travel
Book flights through the travel desk at least 14 days ahead. Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
Rail is preferred for trips under 4 hours.

## Equipment
Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT within 24 hours.
Part XK-2041 is the standard docking station; order it through the IT catalogue.

## Leave
Full-time staff get 25 days of annual leave. Request leave at least 2 weeks ahead in the HR system.
Sick leave longer than 3 days needs a doctor's note.

## Security
Use the password manager for all work accounts. Enable two-factor authentication on email and the VPN.
Never share customer data in public channels.
