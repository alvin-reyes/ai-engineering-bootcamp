# Week 0 activities

*Your week at a glance · about 6 to 8 hours · both tracks*

Week 0 is onboarding week (Mon 2026-11-02 to Fri 2026-11-06). The goal: by Friday your tools work, you have run
your first build, and your role map is started. Week 1 starts with its first live session on Tuesday 2026-11-10.
Times marked [time] are confirmed in the channel.

## Capstone milestone

Your role map is the first capstone step. The ten tasks you list now are the pool you pick your agent's job
from in Week 2.

## Optional stretch

- Start the role agent canvas (`role-agent-canvas.md`, section 01 ROLE) for the task you find most promising.
- Builder: run the prework code with a failing test on purpose and read the pytest output.
- Operator: rewrite one of your real instructions using the five parts from reading 9 and compare the results.
- Try `claude` on a small real file in your own project folder and note one thing it did well and one it got wrong.

## If you're stuck

Post in the channel (Discord) with what you tried and what you saw. Ask early: setup problems are the most common
reason to fall behind, and they are quick to fix when we see them on day 1 or 2. There is an office hour for setup
problems on Day 2–3 [time]. Written questions help everyone who hits the same wall.
