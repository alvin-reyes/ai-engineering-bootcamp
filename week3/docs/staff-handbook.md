# Staff handbook (Brightwater Cycles, made-up sample data)

## Annual leave
Full-time staff get 22 days of paid annual leave per year. Request leave at least 2 weeks ahead
with your team lead. Unused days carry over until 31 March.

## Staff discounts
Staff get 20 percent off bikes and 30 percent off accessories. The discount cannot be combined
with other offers. Family members get half of the staff discount.
